How to play

Enter a four-letter word that changes exactly one letter of the last word. Keep letters in the same positions. Each word must be in our expanded word-game dictionary. TYEE is the special destination.

No swaps. You may replay an earlier word; each replay adds a move. Invalid guesses cost no moves. Undo and reset are always available. A longer solution still wins!

Two bridge words to know: THEE means “you” in older English; a TYKE is a small child.
